Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Porch Pile is a product of YOUR COMPANY NAME ("we," "us"). This policy explains what we collect and why. We keep it short because we collect little.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, a securely hashed password, your timezone (so streaks and due dates land on the right day), and your daily pace goal.
  • Your content: the products, videos, brand deals, coupons, and income figures you add to the app, including any brand contact name, email, or social handle you save on a deal. This is yours and we process it only to provide the service. Your income data is never used for marketing or shared with anyone.
  • Billing data: if you buy Pro, your Stripe customer ID, subscription ID, subscription status, and the date your Pro access runs through. We never see or store your card number.
  • Technical basics: standard server logs (IP, timestamp) needed to run the service securely and prevent abuse. To stop abuse like password guessing, we also keep short-lived rate-limit records that can hold your IP address, your email address, or your account ID. They're deleted automatically within two days.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not use your income or content data for advertising.
  • We do not add you to a marketing newsletter from this app.

Service providers

We use Cloudflare (hosting, database, edge security, the "I'm human" check on signup, and Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies or local storage and doesn't fingerprint visitors), Resend (transactional email such as verification and password-reset messages), and, for paid subscriptions, Stripe (payment processing). These providers process data only to deliver their part of the service.

Cookies and browser storage

We use a single strictly-necessary cookie to keep you logged in (an HttpOnly session cookie). No advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

The app also saves a few small items in your browser's storage. Each one is needed for the app to work the way you'd expect, and none is sent anywhere unless noted:

  • pp_aff_ref: the referral code from an affiliate link, if you arrived through one. It's passed to Stripe when you buy Pro so the right affiliate gets credit, and it's removed once your upgrade goes through. A companion key, pp_aff_ref_at, records when you clicked, and both are dropped 60 days after your last affiliate link click.
  • pp_tour_done: remembers that you've finished or skipped the welcome tour, so it doesn't show again.
  • pp_pace_asked: remembers that the app already asked you for a daily pace goal, so it doesn't ask again.
  • pp_draft_ items (session storage): text you're typing into a form, so it survives a reload or the Back button. They live only in that browser tab and are cleared when you save or close the tab. Passwords are never saved this way.

Do Not Track

Some browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal. Porch Pile doesn't use advertising or tracking tools that follow you across other websites, so it works the same whether or not your browser sends that signal. We don't let any third party collect information about your activity on Porch Pile to track you across other sites over time.

Children

Porch Pile isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email privacy@example.com and we'll delete it.

Your rights

You can export all of your data at any time from your account, and you can permanently delete your account and your data from within the app (see Data retention below for the few records we keep). If you're in the EEA/UK or California, you have rights to access, correct, delete, and port your data. Contact us to exercise them. See also our Do Not Sell or Share page.

Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your account and everything you added to the app (products, videos, brand deals, coupons, income figures, badges, and imports) are hard-deleted from our database.

A few things are kept after you delete your account:

  • Payment records: if you ever paid for Pro, we keep a record of each payment event for tax and accounting. Each record holds an internal account ID, the Stripe event ID, the type of event (for example, a paid or failed invoice), the amount, the currency, and the date. It doesn't include your email address or your app content. Stripe also keeps its own record of your payments under its own privacy policy.
  • Rate-limit records: these clear on their own within two days.
