Accessibility

Effective August 7, 2026

We want Porch Pile to be usable by everyone. We build toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

What we do

  • Color contrast that meets AA for text and interactive elements.
  • Full keyboard operability with visible focus states.
  • Labels and ARIA attributes on form controls and interactive components.
  • Support for prefers-reduced-motion: animations and celebrations are reduced or removed when you set that preference.
  • Responsive layouts that work down to small mobile screens.

Ongoing work

Accessibility is never "done." If you hit a barrier using Porch Pile, please tell us and we will prioritize a fix.
