Questions

Fair questions, plain answers

What is Porch Pile?
A tracker built for Amazon Influencer Program creators. It keeps your products, video queue, brand deals, coupon codes, and monthly income in one place, with a film-next queue, follow-up reminders, and a rank ladder that makes posting videos feel like a game.
Is there a free plan?
Yes. The free plan includes 30 items, 15 videos, 3 brand deals in play, and your current plus previous month of income. No card needed to sign up.
How much does Pro cost?
Pro is $12 a month or $120 a year, which comes out to 2 months free. Pro removes every cap and keeps your full income history. Both plans renew automatically until you cancel, and you can cancel anytime from Settings.
How do I keep track of Amazon influencer products I haven't filmed yet?
Pick one place, put every box in it the day it lands, and give it a status you keep current. The oldest product is the one that slips, since it stops being visible once newer boxes stack on top of it. Porch Pile points the film queue at the oldest thing waiting, so the backlog surfaces on its own instead of waiting for you to remember it.
Can I just use a spreadsheet?
Yes, and plenty of creators do. A sheet holds up fine until the tabs multiply and it stops getting opened. If your sheet is still working for you, keep it. If you want to bring it over later, Porch Pile imports a CSV from Airtable, Google Sheets, Excel or Notion, and the file is read on your device rather than uploaded anywhere.
How do I keep up with brand deals and follow-ups?
The hard part is the waiting, because a brand deal sits in your DMs between the yes and the product actually showing up. Porch Pile keeps each deal in play with a follow-up reminder, so a conversation doesn't go cold while you wait on a box. The free plan holds 3 deals in play at a time.
How do I track income when it comes from so many places?
Amazon influencer income lands in pieces: onsite commissions, Creator Connections, bounties, brand fees, and whatever else you have running. Porch Pile keeps those as separate streams inside one month, and you type the totals in yourself, which takes about a minute.
What if I already have a pile and I'm behind?
Start where you are. Add what's physically in front of you, set each one to its real status, and let the queue sort the order. You don't have to reconstruct months of history to make it useful, and if your history does live in a sheet, you can import it later.
Does Porch Pile connect to my Amazon account?
No. There's no Amazon integration and no scraping. You type your monthly totals in yourself, which takes about a minute a month. Porch Pile isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon.
Can I export or delete my data?
Yes. You can export everything as JSON from Settings anytime, and deleting your account permanently removes all of your data.
Who built Porch Pile?
Porch Pile is open-source software by Jen Soltys of Jen Built It.
